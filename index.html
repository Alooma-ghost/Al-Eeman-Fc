Club History

Al-Eeman Football Club was founded in 2003 in Iwo, Osun State, Nigeria, with a mission to develop young football talents and promote sportsmanship in the community. The name "Al-Eeman" means "Faith" in Arabic, reflecting the club's core values of faith, discipline, and integrity.

From humble beginnings as a local community team, Al-Eeman FC has grown to become one of the most respected football clubs in Osun State. Our youth academy has produced several players who have gone on to play in the Nigerian Professional Football League and beyond.

In 2015, the club achieved a major milestone by winning the Osun State Football Championship, and in 2019, we were runners-up in the Southwest Regional Tournament. Our home ground is the Iwo Township Stadium, where we host matches and train our players.

Beyond football, Al-Eeman FC is committed to community development, organizing charity events, and promoting education among young athletes. We believe in nurturing well-rounded individuals who excel both on and off the pitch.
